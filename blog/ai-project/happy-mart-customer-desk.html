Happy Mart Customer Desk

A customer service desk for an online electronics store where a router sends each question to the department team that owns it, and code checks every reply before the customer sees it.

Lee Yih VenAI Project

A customer service desk for Happy Mart, a fictional online electronics store in Shah Alam with 1,224 products and 2,597 orders over two months. A router sends each part of a customer's question to the department team that owns it, and code checks every reply before the customer sees it.

Customers ask where an order is, whether they can return it, when a refund comes and why they paid for delivery. Each answer belongs to a different department, and each has to come from the store's own rules and the customer's own order.

The desk runs on Claude through an MCP server with 13 resources (six department policies with 28 clauses, a router workbook, five role sheets, the contact list) and 17 tools:

  • Router (Sonnet 5.5): verifies the order ID and the last 4 phone digits, then asks only the teams the question needs.
  • Five department teams: Fulfilment, Returns, Finance, Claims and Marketing, each a sub-agent with its own policy, its own tools and its own slice of the order.
  • Writer (Haiku 5.5): turns the teams' answers into one reply, one paragraph per question.
  • Two shared hooks and a pre-send check: identity, order access and every date window are checked in code, and each draft is read for amounts, dates, handovers and claims the policies never make.

The interesting part is where the decisions sit. Arjun reports that the 27-inch monitor from HM-100110 arrived with a cracked screen. The claims team confirms it is 19 hours since delivery, inside the 48-hour window (QP1), and prepares damage report CL-0001 for Jessica Chan, the claims officer, with no request for photos.

Kumar asks why he paid delivery on an order of exactly RM50 on a campaign day. Code works out that free delivery starts above RM50 (MP3), so the RM5 fee applied, and the reply says so with the clause.

Staff keep every decision about money, returns and claims; the desk prepares each request for a named person. Every recorded test run replays on the two-phone demo.
