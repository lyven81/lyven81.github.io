Kiddoz Back-to-School Campaign Team

A campaign team for a school uniform shop where AI agents write every channel asked for at the same time, code checks each copy against the price list, and the owner approves every channel.

Lee Yih VenAI Project

A campaign team of AI agents for Kiddoz Uniform House, a school uniform shop in Bangi where the owner, Puan Zarina Ahmad, writes every promotion with no marketing staff. One back-to-school campaign runs across WhatsApp, Instagram, email, a blog post and Google search, all in Bahasa Melayu. A wrong price, a date outside the promotion or a promise the shop cannot keep reaches every parent who reads it.

The team runs on the Claude Agent SDK with Claude Haiku 5.5. A coordinator reads the brief and starts one writer for each channel asked for, all in the same response, so they work in parallel. An in-process MCP server holds the shop's price list of 100 campaign items, its store profile and its channel guide. The work splits three ways:

  • AI writes and reviews: each writer knows only the checked facts and its own channel's rules; an independent reviewer that wrote none of the copy reads every channel.
  • Code checks: no writer starts until extract_facts has checked the brief against the price list, and a hook checks every RM figure, percentage, date and product name in each copy as it returns.
  • The owner decides: Puan Zarina answers any question about a price or date, and approves each channel. The system posts nothing.

When Puan Zarina asks for a WhatsApp message with the boys' white primary school shirt at RM18, the price list says RM20.00. Code holds the work and Puan Zarina gets one question. The answer is to use RM20.00, the copy is written and checked, and after one correction from the reviewer it waits for Puan Zarina's approval.

A copy that fails goes back to its own writer with the exact error, at most twice, and the channels that passed stay as they are. In the recorded runs, writing five channels together took 40.8 seconds against 120.7 seconds one after another.

The pattern that keeps showing up: when every figure must trace to the price list, the agents can write freely and the facts still hold.

The demo replays all 21 recorded runs: the facts, each writer's timing, every check and the full copy.
