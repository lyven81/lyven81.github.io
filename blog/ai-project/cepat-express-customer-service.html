Cepat Express Customer Service

A courier customer service agent where the AI answers parcel status from the latest scan, code holds every limit, the phone match and every route, and the Customer Service Lead reviews every handover.

Lee Yih VenAI Project

A customer service agent for Cepat Express, a courier serving Ipoh, Johor Bahru and Klang. Most messages ask where a parcel is, and each answer comes from the latest scan. Delays, missed deliveries and damaged parcels need a person, and each one has to reach the right city with the facts attached, while personal details stay hidden from anyone who cannot show the parcel is theirs.

The agent is one Claude agent with four tools, served through an MCP server that also carries the courier policy, the customer service policy, the delivery status guide and the staff directory. The work splits three ways:

  • AI answers and picks a reason: it reads the status, writes at most two short sentences, and when a person is needed it picks one of 16 fixed reasons.
  • Code keeps control: the status comes from the tracking number alone. The recipient, the address and any case the customer opens need the phone on the parcel to match, and three misses lock that parcel. Code computes the limits from the last scan (3 days with no scan, 48 hours at a hub, out for delivery past 9 pm), picks the city and the person, and writes every handover reply in English, Malay or Mandarin.
  • People decide: Nurul Aina, Customer Service Lead, reviews every handover and its note before a Logistics Officer acts on it.

A customer says the app shows delivered but the parcel never came. The phone matches, the AI picks the reason delivered but not received, and code names May, the Logistics Officer for Ipoh, while the customer hears it was passed to customer service for the Ipoh team. A parcel 60 hours at the Klang hub is flagged by code itself, for Kavita in Klang, the city of the last scan, though the parcel is going to Ipoh.

The pattern that keeps showing up: when code owns the limits, the match and the route, the AI can stay brief and every problem still lands with the one person who can act on it.
