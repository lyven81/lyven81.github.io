Kereta Sewa Jalan-jalan Car Rental Agent

A car rental agent that prices, books and records deposits from the written policy, with confirmations, delivery charges and refunds kept for staff.

Lee Yih VenAI Project

A car rental agent for Kereta Sewa Jalan-jalan, a fictional company renting 56 cars at KLIA. It quotes prices, takes booking requests and records deposit receipts from the written Rental Policy, while confirmations, delivery charges and refunds stay with staff.

Customers message all day about prices, free cars, deposits and cancellations, in English, Malay or Mandarin. Staff check every payment by hand, and every booking waits on that check.

The agent runs on Claude through an MCP server. The Rental Policy (48 numbered clauses), the Car Inventory and the fleet schedule are resources it reads, and nine tools hold the rules in code:

  • check_availability: pickup and return 9am to 6pm, at least 2 hours, at most 14 days, and the price worked out from the rates with the caps in clauses R12 to R14.
  • request_booking: checks the car is still free at the moment of writing, blocks it, and issues the reference with the RM 100 deposit and the DuitNow details.
  • verify_booking: the booking reference and the last 4 mobile digits must match before any detail is shown; three wrong tries lock the check.
  • report_payment: compares an uploaded receipt with the booking in code. The booking stays pending until staff find the money.
  • hand_to_staff: 25 fixed reasons, from a same-day rental to an accident on the road, each in eight fields staff can act on.

Every reply passes through reply_to_customer, which refuses a clause that does not exist, a price no tool returned, or a claim of a handover nobody recorded.

The interesting part is where the decisions sit. Confirm, reject, set the delivery charge and confirm a cancellation are buttons on the staff phone, and each click sends a fixed message back into the customer's chat.

A customer who asks for an Alphard from tomorrow 10am to Friday 10am at KLIA gets request KS-2049 at RM 1,400, with the RM 100 deposit and the DuitNow details, pending until staff confirm the payment.
