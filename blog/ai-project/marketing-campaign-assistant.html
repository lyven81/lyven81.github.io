Comel Diaper Marketing Campaign Assistant

A campaign assistant for a baby diaper retailer where the AI explains each month's results on five platforms, code works out every figure and enforces the budget limits, and the owner decides every change.

Lee Yih VenAI Project

A campaign assistant for Comel Diaper, a baby diaper retailer whose owner runs the advertising alone on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and XiaoHongShu. Each platform reports its own figures, and next month's budget is set without one fixed way to compare them. Once the next month starts, checking last month's changes against the results takes a separate effort.

The assistant is one Claude agent with four tools, served the same way to Claude Code through an MCP server that also carries the business profile, the metric definitions, the review policy and the planning rules. The work splits three ways:

  • AI explains and proposes: it reads the results, explains them in plain words and suggests a budget change or a small test.
  • Code keeps control: every figure, from ROAS (sales for each ringgit of ad spend) to cost per order, is worked out in tools.js with a tag naming its measure, campaign and dates. Code gives each platform a review status, holds each campaign's weekly budget within 20% a period, and caps a test at RM 500 and all tests at RM 1,000.
  • The owner decides: every proposal is saved as waiting. Only the owner approves it, changes the platform and records what was done.

When the owner asks whether XiaoHongShu is doing better than TikTok, code marks XiaoHongShu as Limited results: 25 orders in the last four weeks, below the 30 needed to judge whether results are steady. With no owner targets set, the assistant offers a capped test instead of a budget increase, such as RM 70 to RM 80 a week on campaign XH-05, RM 40 in all, saved for the owner.

After the period closes, the follow-up keeps the owner's record apart from the data. TikTok campaign TT-13 was recorded as paused on 30 September, yet code finds spend of RM 233.15, RM 239.77 and RM 235.43 in the weeks counted, so the assistant reports a conflict and suggests checking the campaign on the platform.

The pattern that keeps showing up: when code owns every figure and every limit, the AI can explain freely and each number still traces to its source.
