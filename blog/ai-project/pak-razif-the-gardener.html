Pak Razif The Gardener

A gardener's own assistant that answers enquiries and books free site visits while he works, with prices and decisions kept for him.

Lee Yih VenAI Project

A site visit assistant for Pak Razif, who runs a two-person gardening service in Shah Alam. It answers customers' routine enquiries and books free site visits into his calendar while he works in other people's gardens.

Customers message about coverage, free visits and prices while his hands are on a hedge trimmer, and a reply that comes hours later can lose the job. Prices and the job scope still have to come from him, because he quotes only after seeing the garden.

The assistant runs on Claude through an MCP server. Six resources hold 103 numbered rules, from the list of services to the booking window, and six tools carry the rules in code:

  • check_coverage: decides by 5-digit postcode, never by a town name; nearby and outstation postcodes go to him.
  • find_open_slots: reads his calendar fresh on every call and returns open slots only, never who holds the others.
  • book_site_visit: checks the slot again when saving, refuses a second visit to the same address, and issues the reference.
  • forward_photo: sends garden photos to him by ID. The model never sees the image, so it never measures or describes one.
  • hand_to_pak_razif: nine fixed reasons, from a price request to a complaint, each with a summary he can act on between jobs.

His phone page opens on that inbox, one card at a time, with Call and WhatsApp buttons. His calendar sits beside it: he blocks any slot he cannot keep and opens any blocked one, field work included, and the assistant offers only what is open at that moment.

The interesting part is where the prices live. There are none in the data and no tool that quotes one, so a request for a rough figure with transport and tax gets the approved reply, an offer of a free site visit, and a note in his inbox.

A customer who asks for Tuesday 8am with a garden photo attached gets one reply: the visit confirmed for Tue 20 Oct, 08:00 to 08:30, with reference HG-1045, and the photo passed to Pak Razif.
