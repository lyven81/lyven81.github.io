AI Project

Wee Auto Car Care

A customer service assistant for a car workshop, with staff in control

Lee Yih VenAI Project

A customer service assistant for an independent car workshop, built over the owner's booking dashboard. It answers prices, free slots and booking questions, and takes booking requests that staff confirm.

A workshop with three bays and three mechanics gets the same questions all day: how much is an oil change, is Thursday free, when is my car ready. The owner answers each one from the dashboard, between jobs. Some answers need a person, such as a bill or a release time, and one customer's booking must never reach another customer.

The assistant runs on Claude through an MCP server. It reads three resources and acts through five tools:

  • Resources: the policy guide, the FAQ and service-packages.json, the only prices it may quote.
  • verify_customer: the booking ID and the last 4 phone digits must both match, and three wrong tries lock the check.
  • check_availability and request_booking: free slots by bay, mechanic skill and job length, with every request saved as pending.
  • escalate_to_staff: one of eight fixed reasons, with a summary staff can act on.

The rules sit in tools.js, shared by the MCP server, the test runner and the web pages. No tool confirms, cancels or charges, so those actions stay with the owner, one click away on the dashboard, where pending requests and handovers sit above the bay schedule.

A question such as "how much to replace my windscreen?" shows the line in practice. Windscreen work is not a package, so the assistant passes it to staff with the reason and the customer's details instead of estimating a price.

The pattern that keeps showing up: a rule the assistant must never break belongs in the tool, where no wording in a conversation can move it.

The demo replays every recorded test conversation without a key, and runs live with your own.
