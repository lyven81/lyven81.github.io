AI Project

Sang Kancil Specialist Clinic

A staff assistant for a paediatric clinic where Claude chooses the tool and shared hooks apply who may see and do what, sending each decision to the named person.

Lee Yih VenAI Project

A staff assistant for Sang Kancil Specialist Clinic, a children's clinic in Selangor where three doctors and a clerk share one assistant across 120 patients. Claude chooses which of eight tools to call, and code decides what each person may see and do.

Each staff member has different rights over records, prescriptions, bookings and bills. Dr Aisyah Rahman leads the clinic and sees infants, Dr Daniel Ong sees ages 1 to 12, Dr Priya Nair sees teenagers, and Nurul Huda runs the desk. Those rights change with a child's age, a consult and the lead doctor's role, and one slip shows a child's record to the wrong person.

The assistant runs on Claude Haiku 5.5 through an MCP server. The clinic's four policies, Fee Schedule, Clinic Formulary, Immunisation Schedule and Clinic Hours and Rota are resources it reads. Around the tools sit three layers of code:

  • PreToolUse hook: checks who is signed in, which patient and which view, and on a prescription the doctor's authority before the formulary, weight, dose and allergy checks.
  • PostToolUse hook: keeps only the fields that person may see, works out vaccination status and amounts owed, and writes the escalation sentence.
  • Back end: checks the critical rules again on its own.

The interesting part is where the work goes next. When Nurul asks for a repeat inhaler for Ryan, 7, the request goes to Dr Daniel, and the reply is one sentence written in code: "Sent to Dr Daniel Ong, associate paediatrician." Dr Daniel's chat then opens with a notice saying what was sent, and nothing is issued until Dr Daniel decides.

When Dr Aisyah asks for the diagnosis of Aiman, Dr Daniel's patient, the reply declines politely and names who has access: "Only Aiman's attending doctor, Dr Daniel Ong, and the clinic clerk can access it."

The same eight tools give each of the four staff a different answer, and the clinic desk replays every question as each of them.
