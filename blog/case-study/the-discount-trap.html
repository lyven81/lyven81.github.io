The Discount Trap

How cutting price quietly drains delivery profit

Lee Yih VenCase Study

A burger and pizza shop selling on the food-delivery apps looked healthy on the order screen, close to 1,000 burger orders and 470 pizza orders a day. The profit underneath told a different story.

On a delivery platform the app takes about 30% of every sale, and discounts pile on top. A full year of the shop's daily book, 730 product-days, shows how much that changes the picture once the money is counted all the way down to what is left after the app is paid.

After the app's cut, the busy product is the weaker earner. Burger clears about RM 6.60 of profit per order; pizza clears RM 14.60, more than double, on less than half the orders. The two lines finish the day at almost the same profit, but burger gets there with double the kitchen work and double the food cost.

Discounting is the bigger leak. Holding traffic, ads and season steady, being seen in the app drives orders about eight times harder than cutting price, yet every ringgit of discount both costs money and shrinks the order it wins. The profit-maximizing discount sits at the very bottom of the range the shop has tried; stepping burger's discount down toward that floor is worth about RM 2,260 a day, roughly RM 826,000 a year.

The menu splits just as cleanly. The Classic Chicken burger and Margherita pizza sell the most but earn the least per plate; the Mushroom Swiss burger and Meat Lovers pizza sell less but earn more. Two items, the Double Cheeseburger and the Seafood pizza, cost so much to make that a deep promotion tips them toward a loss.

The pattern that keeps showing up: on a platform that skims a third off the top, order count is the wrong scoreboard. Profit per order is the number that decides survival, and the busy, discounted product is usually the weaker business.

The recommendation is to fight for visibility instead of buying orders with discounts, cut the discount that is bleeding profit, and build the menu around the plates that actually earn once the app is paid.
