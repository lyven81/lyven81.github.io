Notes from recent case studies and AI projects. Each entry pairs with a full report or live demo. The 10 most recent of each type are listed here.

AI Projects

  • Property Lead Tracker (lead attribution) — Five lead channels, 1,140 enquiries: which one signs tenants, and at what cost.
  • Motor Claim Triage and QC Evaluator (fraud QC). Sorts every claim into four categories with a fixed rule, then scores the fraud flags on real outcomes.
  • Borrower Risk Evaluator (loan-default classifier) — Classifies real loans and scores its own accuracy live against the recorded outcomes.
  • Market Research Agent (governed location intelligence). Answers 10 shop-owner questions from a data pack and cached Maps, near-zero cost.
  • Lucy — Bloom House Concierge (governed florist agent) — Resolves routine asks, escalates sensitive cases to a human, declines the rest.
  • Klinik Dr Fang (governed clinic assistant) — Front-desk AI finds patterns over patient records but structurally can't leak data.
  • Bookshelf (multi-agent inventory planner) — Four ADK agents plan inventory for Malaysian bookstores using deterministic pandas tools.
  • Jade Star Chinese Dining 吉星饭厅 (CNY Reservation, WebMCP) — Bilingual CNY booking page with 7 typed tools agents can call directly.
  • Back-to-School Campaign (BYO-key copywriting agency) — Single brief generates BM copy across WhatsApp, Instagram, email, blog, SEO.
  • Ecommerce Analyst (private AI BI) — Private AI sends an e-commerce seller a one-page brief weekly.
