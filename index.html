About

A short story.

Lee Yih Ven helps business owners make decisions with their data.

The work usually starts with a simple question: Which products are actually making money? Which customers are likely to leave? How much inventory should we prepare next week? Is a dividend sustainable? Behind each question is messy data, competing explanations, and uncertainty. The goal is to turn that uncertainty into a clear answer that someone can act on today.

The solution depends on the problem. Sometimes it is a dashboard. Sometimes it is an analysis. Sometimes it is an AI agent or an automated workflow. The technology changes, but the purpose stays the same: help business owners spend less time searching for answers and more time making decisions.
