About

A short story.

Lee Yih Ven helps business owners make decisions with their data.

The work usually starts with a simple question: which products are actually making money, which customers are likely to leave, how much inventory to prepare next week. Behind each is messy data and uncertainty; the goal is a clear answer someone can act on today.

Sometimes the solution is a dashboard, sometimes an analysis, sometimes an AI agent. The technology changes, but the purpose stays the same.
