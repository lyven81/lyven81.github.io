Hello, I'm Lee.

Every Business Has Work That Could Be Easier

Answering the same customer questions, checking documents, and preparing reports take time away from more important work. These are everyday problems that practical AI applications can help solve.

I build AI tools that handle routine enquiries, process information, and support business decisions. The goal is not to replace people, but to make their work easier and give them more time for tasks that need their attention.

With experience in insurance claims, e-commerce, and AI development, I understand both business processes and technology. Let's explore how AI can help your team work better.

How I Work

1. Understand the process

  • The problem and who it costs
  • The scope of a first version
  • What the AI may and may not do
  • How success will be measured

2. Design the workflow

  • Which tasks the AI handles
  • Which follow fixed rules
  • When a person takes over
  • Which data the system may read

3. Test before trust

  • Valid requests
  • Unauthorised access
  • Outdated information
  • System failures

4. Prepare for launch

  • Security review
  • A named owner for monitoring
  • A rollback plan
  • Approval before real users
